Preserve the evidence

  • ✓ Save screenshots, transaction IDs, timestamps and suspicious messages.
  • ✓ Do not delete evidence or forward suspicious links to others.
  • ✓ Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords or remote-access codes.
  • ✓ Keep complaint/reference numbers for follow-up.
